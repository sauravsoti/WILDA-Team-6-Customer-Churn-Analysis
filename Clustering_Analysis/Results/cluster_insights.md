# Cluster Interpretation and Actionable Insights

## Cluster 0 - Lower-Cost Lower-Churn Customers
- Customers: 2278
- Average tenure: 29.1 months
- Average monthly charges: $27.96
- Observed churn rate: 13.0%
- Fiber optic share: 44.6%
- Month-to-month share: 54.1%

This group combines relatively low monthly charges with below-average observed churn. The business can focus on maintaining service satisfaction and identifying suitable value-added offers without disrupting this comparatively stable group.

## Cluster 1 - Newer Higher-Churn Customers
- Customers: 2534
- Average tenure: 12.7 months
- Average monthly charges: $75.81
- Observed churn rate: 48.3%
- Fiber optic share: 42.0%
- Month-to-month share: 55.3%

This group has the shortest average tenure and the highest observed churn rate. It is the strongest retention-priority segment. Useful actions could include stronger onboarding, early-life customer check-ins, contract conversion offers, and targeted reviews of service value.

## Cluster 2 - Established Higher-Cost Lower-Churn Customers
- Customers: 2231
- Average tenure: 58.0 months
- Average monthly charges: $89.78
- Observed churn rate: 15.6%
- Fiber optic share: 45.5%
- Month-to-month share: 55.7%

This group has the longest average tenure and higher monthly charges while maintaining a comparatively low observed churn rate. The business should protect loyalty in this high-value established segment through service quality, recognition, and carefully targeted retention or loyalty offers.
